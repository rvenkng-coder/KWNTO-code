# Accounts and Teams

## Sign up

Go to **Sign up**, enter your email, name and a password (at least 10 characters, with a letter and a number). You'll see a message to check your email for a verification link.

## Verify your email

Open the link in the email we send you. Once your address is verified (the page picks this up when you return to it), you can create and join teams.

## Sign in

Go to **Sign in**, enter your email and password. If you forget your password, click **Forgot password?** — if that account exists, a reset link is sent.

After three wrong passwords, you'll see "Try again after 5 minutes." and must wait.

## Create a team

Once verified, click **Create team** and enter a name. You become the team owner.

## Join by code

The owner enables a join code on the team page. Share that code with someone — they enter it under **Join with code** and are added to the team.

## Invites

The owner can invite people by email. The invitee sees the invite in their inbox and can accept or decline. The owner never sees the invitee's email — only their name once they've accepted.

## Light and dark theme

The button in the top bar switches between the light and the dark theme. Your choice is saved to your account, so it is the same after a reload and on every device you sign in on. A new account starts dark. On the sign-in screen, before you are signed in, the app follows your browser or system setting.

## What only the owner can do

- Enable/disable the join code and regenerate it
- Invite people by email
- Remove members
- Delete the team

Projects, uploads, comments and share links come in later releases.
