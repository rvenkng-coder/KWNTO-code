# Playing the story (Player Mode)

Player Mode plays your story one node at a time, the way a reader or a player would meet it. Use it to playtest the pacing and the choices, and share a link so others can try it.

## Opening it

- **In the editor:** choose **Play** in the title bar, next to **Share**.
- **On a share link:** a person who opens a **view** or **comment** link sees **Play** beside the project name. They do not need an account for a view link. (An **edit** link opens the editor, which has its own **Play**.)

The player covers the whole window. **Close** or Escape returns you to where you were.

## What you see

Play starts at the project's **start node** (the first node you made, unless you chose another in its panel). Each screen shows:

- the **chapter name**, when you start and whenever the story moves into a different chapter;
- the node's **title**;
- its **script lines** in screenplay style: the character's name, then what they say; a line with no name is action or narration and shows in italics;
- its **images**, **audio** and **video**. Audio and video start with the node and stop when you move on;
- its **choices** as buttons. A choice shows its text; if you left the text empty it shows the title of the node it leads to.

When no choice leads anywhere, the screen says **The End**.

**Not shown to players:** cues, lighting notes, comments and the links list. Those are for the crew.

## Moving around

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| A choice button | Goes to the node it leads to; the new screen slides in from the right |
| **Back** | Takes one step back; the screen slides in from the left. Disabled on the first screen |
| **Restart** | Goes back to the start node and forgets the path; the screen fades in |
| **History** | Shows or hides the path so far: the first node, then each choice taken and where it led |
| **Close** (or Escape) | Leaves the player |

If you set your system to reduce motion, the screens change without moving.

## A choice that is not linked yet

A choice you wrote but never linked still appears as a button, so you can see it. Clicking it does not move; it says `This choice isn't linked to a node yet.` Link it in the editor (see [Choices](editor.md#choices-on-the-card)) or delete it. A choice that leads to a node in the trash is not shown at all.

## Good to know

- **The story is read when you open the player.** If you or a teammate change the project while it is open, the open player does not change under you. Close it and open it again to see the changes. A live share link works the same way: it refreshes the map every 30 seconds, but an open player keeps the story it started with.
- **Nothing is saved or counted.** Playing changes nothing in the project, and your choices are not recorded.
- **No start node.** An empty project, or one whose start node is in the trash, says `This project has no start node, so there is nothing to play yet.` Add a node, or make a node the start in its panel.
- **Media that cannot load** shows `Media removed` in its place. If a file loads but your computer cannot play it (for example, there is no audio device), the player stays.
